# Glowing Nodes

### Ore veins, herbs and hides that glow in WoW Forever.

[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/glowing-nodes) · [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/glowing-nodes) · [Discord](https://discord.gg/cqAVWpeVvd)

Glowing Nodes makes the node you are about to gather glow in the world, in a colour of your choice for ores, herbs and skinnable corpses, with the profession level it needs on top. A soft sound and a RareScanner-style alert card tell you what you found.

## Features

- A bright glow on the node: halo, turning ring, rays, rising glints and an optional pillar of light.
- The level it needs, in the game's difficulty colours, and optionally your own.
- A soft sound for each kind, and an alert card with the node's icon, name and level.
- Learns the level of Forever's new nodes as you play.
- Nine languages: English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean and Chinese.

## How it works

WoW doesn't let addons see the nodes around you. The glow hangs on the nameplate the game gives to the target of its Interact Key (soft targeting), so one node glows at a time: the one you are about to reach. The addon turns the Interact Key on for you and restores your settings if you switch it off.

## Install

Get it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/glowing-nodes) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/glowing-nodes), or extract the `GlowingNodes` folder from a [release](https://github.com/KullThran/GlowingNodes/releases) into your Forever client's `Interface/AddOns` folder.

Type `/gn` in game to open the options.
